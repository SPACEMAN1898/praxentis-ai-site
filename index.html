01 — Function

Automation that watches the work.

Praxentis AI is the intelligence division of the studio. It automates the repetitive, measures what matters, and surfaces insight a busy practice would otherwise miss.

automate

Repetitive clinic work runs on a schedule instead of a to-do list.

report

Client-ready documents and recurring summaries, generated not written.

measure

The numbers a practice runs on, captured consistently over time.

monitor

Quiet, continuous checking — compliance, schedules, drift.

surface insight

Findings arrive as clear decisions, not dashboards to decode.

02 — Flagship

The Compliance Audit Pipeline.

An automated Australian healthcare website compliance audit. Hit run, give it a URL, get back a client-ready report.

STAGE 01

URL in

Point the pipeline at a clinic's website. Playwright crawls and captures the live pages.

STAGE 02

04 — Data policy
“Patient health information does not pass through general-purpose foreign-hosted models.”

This is a hard design constraint, not a marketing line. Praxentis AI systems are architected so that identifiable patient data stays out of foreign-hosted general-purpose models — with Australian infrastructure and Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) alignment as the default posture across the studio.
